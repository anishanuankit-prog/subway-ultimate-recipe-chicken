Ultimate Chicken Sub at Home

Requirements

  • Chicken Breast
  • Lettuce
  • Six Inch Buns
  • Chipotle Powder
  • Fresh Cilantro
  • Spices: Cayenne Powder, Garlic Powder, Cumin, Salt, Paprika, Black Pepper, Oregano
  • Butter
  • Olive oil
  • All-purpose Flour
  • Lime Juice
  • White Beans
  • Garlic Clove
  • Nutritional Yeast
  • Water

Steps

Chicken:

  1. Slice the chicken breasts in half
  2. Pound till one inch thickness
  3. In a small bowl, mix garlic powder, smoked paprika, oregano, salt, pepper, and cayenne powder
  4. Season the chicken breasts with olive oil
  5. Season the chicken breasts with seasoning
  6. Dredge breasts with light coating of all purpose flour and spices
  7. Heat the olive oil in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat
  8. Cook the chicken until golden-brown crust forms
  9. Flip, reduce heat, and cook till juices run clear
  10. Should be 160 - 163° Fahrenheit.
  11. Add butter and take skillet off heat.
  12. Let them rest
  13. If doesn't seem cooked, bake for some time.
  14. Chop them up into small pieces

Lettuce:

  1. Take lettuce and chop them up into small pieces

    2. Chipotle Sauce:

    1. Mix chipotle powder, garlic clove, cumin, salt, lime juice, white beans, water, nutritional yeast and blend until smooth.

    Serving

    1. Cook 6 inch buns
    2. Put chicken on bottom bun, then lettuce, then sauce, and close with top bun
